# Unified Party Hub

## Introduction

Small and medium-sized businesses face recurring challenges when managing customer, supplier, and other party data within ERPNext systems.

{{< hint warning >}}
**Real challenges faced by businesses:**
- The need for **full multi-currency support** in all transactions and reports.
- Difficulty obtaining **unified and accurate financial reports** that connect all types of parties.
- Duplicate party data in multiple places within the system.
- Lack of a **dedicated analytic dimension** to analyze party financial performance.
- **Multiple role types** for a single party (customer + supplier + employee).
- Limited flexibility to apply the same concept in other sectors such as hospitals, associations, or business partners.
{{< /hint >}}

---

## Solution

{{< hint info >}}
**Unified Party Hub** provides a unified framework to manage all parties in a single master record called **Party Master**.  
It merges data and intelligently links it to financial documents, with multi-currency support and duplication removal.
{{< /hint >}}

---

## Key Features

### 🔹 Data Unification
All parties (customer, supplier, employee, partner...) are organized under a **single master record**, ensuring data consistency and preventing duplication.

{{< hint success >}}
**Benefit:** No more duplicate records, with easy tracking of all party transactions from a single place.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 💱 Multi-Currency Support
A dedicated mechanism manages multiple currencies in documents and reports, with optimized indexes on `(party_master, currency)`.

{{< hint success >}}
**Benefit:**  
Party reports can be displayed in their original currency or in the company’s unified currency, providing more accurate financial comparisons.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 📊 Unified and Comprehensive Reports
The app generates unified reports for all parties on a single screen.  
Metrics include invoice counts, balances, and outstanding documents.

{{< hint info >}}
Reports can be filtered easily by company, currency, or party type.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧾 Dedicated Analytic Dimension
You can create an **analytic dimension** linked to Party Master and use it for financial analysis and cost centers.

{{< hint success >}}
**Benefit:** Track party performance as financial analysis centers without the need for new sub-accounts.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧠 Multi Role Type Support
The app allows a single party to have multiple roles (customer + supplier + employee...), automatically managing the links in the background.

{{< hint success >}}
**Benefit:** Flexibility to handle complex parties such as holding companies or commercial intermediaries.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧩 Expandable to Different Sectors
The app is not limited to commercial parties but provides a flexible structure usable in sectors like:

- Hospitals (patients, doctors, insurance companies)
- Nonprofit organizations (donors, beneficiaries)
- Business partners (distributors, agents, sister companies)

{{< hint info >}}
The platform is not limited to a specific type of party but is a general **Master Data Management (MDM) framework**.
{{< /hint >}}

---

## Added Value

| Challenge | Before the App | After Unified Party Hub |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| Multi-currency | Partial and disconnected support | Full support in invoices and reports |
| Party reports | Separate reports for each type | Unified reports for all parties |
| Duplicate data | Duplication between customers and suppliers | Single master record: Party Master |
| Flexibility across sectors | Limited to customers and suppliers | Applicable to any sector |
| Multiple roles | Not supported | Supported for a single party |

---

## Why Unified Party Hub?

{{< hint info >}}
**Unified Party Hub** is the first step toward building a **Master Data Management (MDM) system** within ERPNext.  
It provides flexibility, consistency, multi-currency support, and unified reporting — bringing ERPNext closer to the level of major systems like Oracle and SAP.
{{< /hint >}}
