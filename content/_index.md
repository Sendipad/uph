{{% columns %}}
- ## What Unified Party Hub Is
  A framework to unify all customers, suppliers, employees, and partners into a single **Party Master** record in ERPNext.  
  Reduces duplication, supports multi-currency, and provides unified reports.

- ## What It Is Not
  Not a replacement for ERPNext core. It is an **enhancement module** for cleaner data management.
{{% /columns %}}
