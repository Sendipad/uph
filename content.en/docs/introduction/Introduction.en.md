# Unified Party Hub

## Introduction

Small and medium-sized businesses face recurring challenges when managing customer, supplier, and other party data within ERPNext systems.

{{< hint warning >}}
<strong>Real challenges faced by businesses:</strong>

<ul>
<li>The need for <strong>full multi-currency support</strong> in all transactions and reports.</li>
<li>Difficulty obtaining <strong>unified and accurate financial reports</strong> that connect all types of parties.</li>
<li>Duplicate party data in multiple places within the system.</li>
<li>Lack of a <strong>dedicated analytic dimension</strong> to analyze party financial performance.</li>
<li><strong>Multiple role types</strong> for a single party (customer + supplier + employee).</li>
<li>Limited flexibility to apply the same concept in other sectors such as hospitals, associations, or business partners.</li>
</ul>
{{< /hint >}}

---

## Solution

{{< hint info >}}
<strong>Unified Party Hub</strong> provides a unified framework to manage all parties in a single master record called <strong>Party Master</strong>.<br>
It merges data and intelligently links it to financial documents, with multi-currency support and duplication removal.
{{< /hint >}}

---

## Key Features

### 🔹 Data Unification

All parties (customer, supplier, employee, partner...) are organized under a <strong>single master record</strong>, ensuring data consistency and preventing duplication.

{{< hint success >}}
<strong>Benefit:</strong> No more duplicate records, with easy tracking of all party transactions from a single place.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 💱 Multi-Currency Support

A dedicated mechanism manages multiple currencies in documents and reports, with optimized indexes on <code>(party_master, currency)</code>.

{{< hint success >}}
<strong>Benefit:</strong><br>
Party reports can be displayed in their original currency or in the company’s unified currency, providing more accurate financial comparisons.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 📊 Unified and Comprehensive Reports

The app generates unified reports for all parties on a single screen.<br>
Metrics include invoice counts, balances, and outstanding documents.

{{< hint info >}}
Reports can be filtered easily by company, currency, or party type.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧾 Dedicated Analytic Dimension

You can create an <strong>analytic dimension</strong> linked to Party Master and use it for financial analysis and cost centers.

{{< hint success >}}
<strong>Benefit:</strong> Track party performance as financial analysis centers without the need for new sub-accounts.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧠 Multi Role Type Support

The app allows a single party to have multiple roles (customer + supplier + employee...), automatically managing the links in the background.

{{< hint success >}}
<strong>Benefit:</strong> Flexibility to handle complex parties such as holding companies or commercial intermediaries.
{{< /hint >}}

---

### 🧩 Expandable to Different Sectors

The app is not limited to commercial parties but provides a flexible structure usable in sectors like:

<ul>
<li>Hospitals (patients, doctors, insurance companies)</li>
<li>Nonprofit organizations (donors, beneficiaries)</li>
<li>Business partners (distributors, agents, sister companies)</li>
</ul>

{{< hint info >}}
The platform is not limited to a specific type of party but is a general <strong>Master Data Management (MDM) framework</strong>.
{{< /hint >}}

| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Multi-currency             | Partial and disconnected support            | Full support in invoices and reports |
| Party reports              | Separate reports for each type              | Unified reports for all parties      |

---

## Why Unified Party Hub?

{{< hint info >}}
<strong>Unified Party Hub</strong> is the first step toward building a <strong>Master Data Management (MDM) system</strong> within ERPNext.<br>
It provides flexibility, consistency, multi-currency support, and unified reporting — bringing ERPNext closer to the level of major systems like Oracle and SAP.
{{< /hint >}}
